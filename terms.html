Terms of use

A personal application operated on its owner's Windows PC.

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Maintainer: DriedRack

Purpose & availability

Personal Automation Hub is a personal project for scheduled review of PC clutter and, in development, Gmail messages. This website provides information about that project. It is not a hosted mailbox, a public account-registration service or a commercial service with guaranteed availability. Features marked in development are not yet available.

Authorized accounts & resources

Use the application only with files and accounts you own or are authorized to manage. Google account access is granted through Google's authorization process. Google's terms and account policies continue to apply. The project is independently maintained and is not a Google product or an endorsement by Google.

Review & cleanup choices

Review recommendations before approving cleanup. Age, sender and automated suggestions do not establish that an item is unwanted. PC review items are moved to the Windows Recycle Bin after confirmation. The planned Gmail integration moves confirmed messages to Gmail's Trash. Recovery depends on Windows or Gmail, available data and their retention policies; it is not guaranteed by the Hub.

Starred and important messages are intended to be excluded from bulk selection by default. Their flags do not replace your review. No automatic permanent deletion or email sending is part of the Gmail integration's scope.

Privacy & control

The privacy policy describes the app's local data practices and planned Gmail access. You control your local installation, project backups and connected accounts. Google authorization may expire or be revoked, requiring you to sign in again. Provider limits or account restrictions can interrupt synchronization.

Changes & questions

The personal application and these terms may change as features develop. Material changes to email data use will be disclosed before they take effect. These terms do not override rights or obligations that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

Questions can be raised in the public issue tracker. Include a general description, keeping mailbox contents and credentials private.
