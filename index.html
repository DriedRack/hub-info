A personal project maintained by DriedRack. This website provides app information and policies. The application runs separately on its owner's PC.

This public information site has no mailbox sign-in, email viewer, or connection to your local dashboard.

PC cleanup AVAILABLE LOCALLY

Review old downloads and cohesive folders, protect important locations, and move approved items to the Windows Recycle Bin. Scheduled scans and remembered Keep decisions help future reviews stay manageable.

Gmail review IN DEVELOPMENT

The planned Gmail automation brings multiple accounts into a review queue with sender, subject, short preview and importance indicators. Keep postpones review; confirmed cleanup moves selected messages to Gmail's Trash.

Spam and Trash are excluded from review. Starred and important messages are protected from bulk selection by default. Sending email and permanent deletion are outside this integration's scope.

Account access

When Gmail support becomes available, each account will connect from the local Hub through Google's authorization screen. The requested Gmail permission is gmail.modify, needed to read message information and move approved messages to Trash. Google's permission also grants sending capability; the Hub does not provide a sending feature.
