Privacy policy

Personal review tools, with clear boundaries around your data.

Last updated: October 21, 2026 · Maintainer: DriedRack

Development status. Gmail support is in development. This policy describes its intended operation when enabled. This information website does not connect to mailboxes or collect email messages. Any material change to these practices will be disclosed before that use begins.

What the local application accesses

PC cleanup scans the personal folders selected by its owner. File metadata, relative locations, review decisions and activity history are stored locally. Broad scans keep application and collection folders together, and file contents may be hashed for duplicate detection when applicable.

For each Gmail account you authorize, the planned integration accesses your account email address, Gmail message and conversation identifiers, sender and recipient headers, subject, timestamps, a short message preview, and labels or flags such as Inbox, Promotions, unread, starred, important, Spam and Trash. A preview is part of an email's content. Attachments and full message bodies are not intended to be downloaded for the review list.

The integration requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to retrieve message information and move messages you confirm to Trash. That Google permission also allows additional operations, including sending; the Hub's planned features do not send email or permanently delete messages.

Why this data is used

Mail information is used to show a local review queue, distinguish accounts, open messages in Gmail, highlight protected messages, synchronize changes, resurface kept messages after your chosen interval, and carry out your explicitly confirmed Trash actions. Spam and Trash are excluded from review. Review and cleanup progress are recorded so interrupted operations can be reconciled.

Explicit Keep, Trash, bulk-rule and correction choices may be retained with message context to improve visible, personal review suggestions. A bulk age-based action is distinguished from individual message preferences. Ignoring a message does not count as permission to delete it.

Local storage, retention & backups

Application settings, cached summaries and previews, message identifiers, review choices, sync progress and activity history are intended to stay in the owner's local project folder. Authorization tokens must be protected and encrypted at rest. No centrally hosted Hub mailbox database is part of this design.

Cached mail information is retained for review and synchronization while the account is connected. The Gmail integration will provide account disconnection and removal of its cached mail data and account-specific review history. These controls must be available before mailbox connections are enabled. Local project backups can contain application data and protected credentials; the owner controls those copies and their deletion separately.

Moving a message to Trash does not mean immediate permanent deletion. Gmail controls Trash retention and recovery. Backing up the Hub's configuration is not a backup of the Gmail mailbox.

Sharing & external services

The planned local integration communicates directly with Google for authorization, synchronization and approved mailbox actions. Opening a message transfers you to Gmail in your browser. Email data is not sold, used for advertising, or published on this site. No AI processing of mail is currently enabled. Any future external AI processing would require a separate explanation and your affirmative consent before email data is transferred. Google API data will not be used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Personal Automation Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your controls

You choose which accounts to connect and can revoke access in your Google Account's third-party connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access; it does not remove previously stored local data or backups. Disconnection from the Hub must stop synchronization and invalidate queued account actions. Actual cleanup requires your confirmation, and the current message state must be checked before a Trash action.

This public website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no app analytics scripts, tracking pixels, forms, mailbox data, credentials or advertising. GitHub processes web requests and logs visitor IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your browser may also process information under its own settings. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Questions & policy updates

Contact the maintainer through the public project issue tracker, using a general description without personal email content, passwords or tokens. Material changes will be reflected here and, where account data use changes, disclosed in the local application with renewed consent before that use.
